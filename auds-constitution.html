Preamble

We, the members of the Adamson University Debate Society, in order to foster academic discourse, promote intellectual excellence, develop critical thinking skills, and uphold the values of reasoned argumentation and respectful dialogue, do hereby establish this Constitution to govern our organization and guide our activities in accordance with the principles of academic freedom, diversity, and inclusive excellence.

Article I: Name and Purpose

Section 1: Name

The official name of this organization shall be the "Adamson University Debate Society," hereinafter referred to as "AUDS" or "the Society."

Section 2: Purpose

The purposes of AUDS are to:

  • Promote the art and practice of competitive debate
  • Develop critical thinking and analytical skills among members
  • Foster effective communication and public speaking abilities
  • Encourage academic research and evidence-based argumentation
  • Build leadership capabilities through participation and governance
  • Represent Adamson University in intercollegiate debate competitions
  • Promote civic engagement and democratic participation

Article II: Membership

Section 1: Eligibility

Membership in AUDS is open to all bonafide students of Adamson University, both undergraduate and graduate, who are in good academic standing and demonstrate commitment to the Society's values.

Section 2: Categories of Membership

  • Resident Members: Regular members who have completed the application process and maintain active participation
  • Varsity Members: Elite members selected for competitive representation based on skill and commitment
  • Alumni Members: Former members who maintain association with the Society
  • Honorary Members: Individuals recognized for exceptional contribution to debate and the Society

Section 3: Rights and Responsibilities

All members have the right to:

  • Participate in Society activities and training programs
  • Vote in elections and on matters brought before the membership
  • Hold office subject to eligibility requirements
  • Access Society resources and materials

Article III: Executive Structure

Section 1: Executive Committee

The executive authority of AUDS shall be vested in an Executive Committee consisting of:

  • President: Chief executive officer and external representative
  • Vice President: Assistant to the President and succession officer
  • Secretary: Record keeper and communications officer
  • Treasurer: Financial officer and resource manager
  • Training Director: Educational programs and skill development
  • Competition Director: Tournament coordination and varsity team management

Section 2: Terms of Office

Executive officers shall serve for one academic year and may be re-elected for subsequent terms, provided they maintain good standing and academic eligibility.

Section 3: Election Process

Officers shall be elected by secret ballot during the annual general meeting, with elections conducted according to established procedures and overseen by an independent elections committee.

Article IV: Meetings and Procedures

Section 1: Regular Meetings

AUDS shall hold regular meetings at least twice monthly during the academic year, with schedule and location determined by the Executive Committee.

Section 2: Special Meetings

Special meetings may be called by the President or upon written request of at least one-third of the active membership, with 48 hours advance notice required.

Section 3: Quorum

A quorum for conducting official business shall consist of a simple majority of active members present at a properly called meeting.

Article V: Constitutional Amendments

Section 1: Proposal Process

Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed by the Executive Committee or by petition of at least twenty percent of the active membership.

Section 2: Ratification

Proposed amendments must be approved by a two-thirds majority vote of members present at a meeting called specifically for this purpose, with at least one week advance notice.

Section 3: Effective Date

Approved amendments shall take effect immediately upon ratification unless otherwise specified.
