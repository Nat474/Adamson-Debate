General Debate Rules

1. Respect and Professionalism

  • All participants must maintain respectful discourse at all times
  • Personal attacks, discriminatory language, or harassment are strictly prohibited
  • Disagreements should focus on ideas and arguments, not individuals
  • Professional attire is required for all formal debates and competitions

2. Time Management

  • Strictly adhere to allocated speaking times for each segment
  • Protected time periods must be respected (no interruptions allowed)
  • Preparation time limits must be observed
  • Late arrivals may result in forfeit or penalty

3. Research and Evidence

  • All sources must be credible and verifiable
  • Fabricated or misleading evidence is grounds for disqualification
  • Citations should be provided when requested
  • Use of technology for research must follow tournament guidelines

Debate Formats

Parliamentary Debate

The primary format used in AUDS competitions featuring government and opposition teams with specific roles and time allocations.

Asian Parliamentary Format

Modified parliamentary style adapted for Asian debate circuits with emphasis on substantive arguments and logical reasoning.

Oxford-Style Debate

Traditional format featuring opening statements, rebuttals, and closing arguments with audience voting.

Public Forum Debate

Accessible format designed for general audiences with emphasis on clear communication and current events.

Judging Criteria

Content (40%)

  • Logical reasoning and argument structure
  • Quality and relevance of evidence
  • Depth of analysis and critical thinking
  • Effective refutation of opposing arguments

Style (30%)

  • Clarity and eloquence of delivery
  • Appropriate use of rhetoric and persuasion
  • Confidence and stage presence
  • Effective use of voice and gesture

Strategy (30%)

  • Time management and pacing
  • Tactical awareness and adaptability
  • Team coordination and consistency
  • Overall debate strategy execution
